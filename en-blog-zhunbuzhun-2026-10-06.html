Why Do Some Feng Shui Consultants Get Results — and Others Don't?

The gap is in the practitioner, not in feng shui itself

✦ By Master Rain ✦

✦Let's Be Blunt

Someone told me:"Master Rain, I hired a consultant once. Paid good money. Nothing changed. Does this stuff even work?"

My answer: don't blame feng shui yet. Same craft, wildly different outcomes — the gap is lineage and skill.

Same scalpel: in a master surgeon's hand it saves lives; in a quack's hand it kills.

✦Lineage Gap One: Is the"Wealth Position"They Found Even Real?

Many schools calculate a"wealth position"from their own theory that simply isn't the real one — wrong spot, no result. That's not feng shui failing; that's the practitioner missing the target.

Nearly a thousand years of Tianxing Feng Shui practice tells us: only a wealth position measured by Tianxing methods is the true wealth position, and only adjustment there produces real wealth activation. Each room, each space has its own wealth position; one space can hold several. Find the true position and work it — that's what moves wealth.

Hiring someone for wealth activation? Ask one question: how do you locate the wealth position? If they can't explain their method, find someone else.

✦Lineage Gap Two: Do They Read Landform, or Birth Charts?

A main door's orientation can't be decided by compass luck, and certainly not by your birth chart — the East-four/West-four life-gua script is one school's theory. I don't use it.

Orientation must be decided by the external environment in full: mountains, water, roads, trees, towers, tall buildings — landform and sha are the foundation. If the practitioner is reading the wrong thing to begin with, how could the adjustment work? Many failed adjustments trace back to exactly this: they worked on something that was never the real influence.

✦Craft Gap One: Field Experience, Not Book Knowledge

Feng shui is a field discipline, not a memorization contest. Some"consultants"recite theory flawlessly and go blank on a real site.

My teacher, Master Zhang Naiyi: fifty years of field practice, over a thousand cases. Our founder, Zhang Jiuyi: contract feng shui — work first, payment after results. Without real skill, who could survive a rule like that? I am the strongest of my teacher's hundred-plus disciples at locating dragon veins and burial sites. Craft can't be faked.

✦Craft Gap Two: The Three Non-Negotiables of Adjustment

Feng shui adjustment needs the right object, at the right time, in the right position — all three, no exceptions.

Everything carries energy. Right position: a beneficial field — wealth, health, relationships activated. Wrong position: a harmful field, a sha that damages wealth and health. Many failed adjustments fail here: the object placed but the timing wrong; the timing right but the position off. A hair's breadth off, a mile off in effect.

✦Craft Gap Three: Honesty About Time

A home adjustment: you'll feel the shift in one to three months. Sheng Ji life-base planting: three to six months. What I promise is results within three years — the time to feel a change, not a fantasy.

Anyone promising"riches in seven days" — enjoy the show. And anyone claiming 100% accuracy is a fraud: too many variables shape a life. Getting the big picture right, most of the time, is what a master does. We read probabilities, not certainties.
