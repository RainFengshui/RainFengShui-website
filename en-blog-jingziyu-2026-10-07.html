Will a Mirror by the Door and a Fish Tank
Bring Wealth? Not That Simple

The object-worship trap is costing you real money

✦The Question

"Master, if I hang a mirror by my door and keep a fish tank, will wealth come?" — I get this question dozens of times a month.

This is what I call "object worship": the belief that buying something and placing it summons money. Order a pixiu online, and express delivery brings you a fortune.

Mirrors have energy, fish have energy, water has energy — but energy pointed at the wrong place is sha.

🪞 Three Classic Object Disasters

Disaster one: mirror facing the door. A mirror reflects energy fields, and the door is the mouth that takes in energy. Walking into a mirror at the entrance reflects the wealth that just arrived right back out. The money never comes in — it's all blocked at the door.

Disaster two: the fish tank becomes a dead tank. Tank water is living-water energy; only alive and thriving counts as wealth. Fish die, water stinks — dead qi plus turbid qi is a heavy sha. If you can't keep fish alive, better not to keep them.

Disaster three: the wealth-object committee. The doorway piled with pixiu, golden toads, treasure bowls, thinking quantity wins. The true wealth position was never found — wherever they're placed it's decoration, and misplaced they turn into sha.

✦First, Understand: What a True Wealth Position Is

Let me correct the biggest misconception: the wealth position is not at the door. The door is the energy mouth — the passage energy enters through. The wealth position is where energy gathers and rests — usually precisely not at the door.

Remember the true meaning of the wealth position: every room, every space has different wealth positions, and one space can even hold several. Only a wealth position measured by Tianxing feng shui is a true wealth position. The bright/dark wealth position talk is wrong. Work on a true wealth position and you get real wealth activation.

Get the wealth position wrong and wherever the mirror shines, wherever the fish swim, wherever the pixiu sits — it's all guessing. Getting it right by guessing is pure luck.

✦Objects Are Energy Switches — but the Switch Must Be Wired to the Right Place

Everything has energy; misplaced it becomes sha

Mirrors reflect qi, fish tanks carry water qi, pixiu and toads each carry their own imagery — these energies are real. Placed right they become auspicious, activating wealth and family fortune; placed wrong they become sha, draining money and inviting illness. That's why I say: objects are a core instrument of feng shui adjustment — but never a wishing well.

The three elements: object, timing, direction

When I actually adjust for wealth, I do this: a custom-made object, installed at a chosen auspicious hour, placed at a direction fixed by on-site survey — all three, none optional. All three present, the object is an energy switch. Missing one, it's a decoration.

So mirrors and fish tanks aren't forbidden — they must be placed only after the directions are surveyed and the position is fixed. Placing them blindly is worse than not placing them.

✦Common Questions

Q: Can a mirror go by the door at all?

Yes, but the direction and facing must be read — fixed by on-site survey. Directly facing the door usually fails: it reflects the entering energy field back out. A turning foyer or a side angle depends on the actual layout. A mirror is a sharp tool, not a decoration; the wrong position injures wealth.

Q: What does keeping fish for wealth require?

A fish tank is a water formation, and water governs wealth — correct — but the position, the size, and the water flow direction must all fit the pattern. Alive and thriving is wealth; dead fish and stinking water — dead qi plus turbid qi — is sha, with not a trace of wealth in it. Can't keep them well, better not to keep them.

Q: Do pixiu and golden toads actually work?

They work, but only on a true wealth position, and the material, the facing, and the timing all have their rules. Buy one casually and set it down — it's a craft ornament. Remember the true meaning of the wealth position: every space's true wealth position is different; only what Tianxing feng shui measures is a true wealth position.

Q: What's the most direct way to adjust for wealth?

Survey on site first, find the true wealth position, then set the remedy plan. Objects are one instrument among several; layout, date selection, and objects working together are the complete plan. Counting on a purchased object to make you rich is paying a stupidity tax.
