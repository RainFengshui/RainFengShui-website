Feng shui isn't magic. Objects are the supporting cast.

✦The Question I Hear Every Week

"Master Rain, I bought a Pixiu statue for my desk six months ago. Where's my fortune? Did I get a fake?"

I hear some version of this constantly. Time to settle it once and for all.

Short answer: the statue isn't the problem. Your understanding of feng shui is. Feng shui is not magic, and objects don't generate wealth on their own.

✦Objects Are the Catalyst, Not the Cure

What is feng shui? Landform (the environment around the building) + energy pattern (how qi moves through the space) + the people inside. All three, together. And objects? A supporting tool in an adjustment — at most 10% of the equation.

Think of it this way: the building is the body, the energy pattern is the circulatory system, and an object is a bandage. If the circulation is blocked, a hundred bandages won't help.

Your company entrance sits in a draining sector. Clients keep slipping away, key staff keep quitting. Will a crystal ball on the reception desk fix that? Of course not. You adjust the entrance and the layout first — the object is the finishing touch, added last. Get the order backwards and you've wasted your money.

✦Wrong Position = Decoration

The same Pixiu placed on the correct wealth sector supports cash flow; placed wrong, it's a paperweight. Get the position wrong and the object equals zero.

This is exactly why I insist on on-site surveys: without compass measurements and a reading of the surrounding landform, any object recommendation is guesswork. Those"five must-have wealth objects for your office"listicles? Entertainment. Anyone prescribing cures without examining your specific environment isn't a consultant — they're a content writer.

Remember the order: read the environment and set the layout first, then discuss adjustments, and only then talk about objects. Skipping the first two steps and buying objects is like taking medicine without a diagnosis.

✦Common Questions

Q: Do feng shui objects like Pixiu actually work?

They can — but only in the right position within the right layout. Objects are a supporting tool in an adjustment, at most 10% of the equation. A Pixiu sitting alone on a desk, expected to generate wealth by itself, is reassurance, not feng shui.

Q: Can I just buy feng shui objects online?

The object itself is rarely the issue — placement is. Without an on-site survey to determine position and read the surrounding landform, any placement advice is guesswork. Generic internet articles ("what Dragons should place this year") are entertainment, not consulting.

Q: Do I need objects to fix negative formations?

Not necessarily. Many problems are solved more thoroughly through layout — repositioning an entrance, adding a screen, rerouting circulation. Objects are the last resort and the finishing touch, never the primary solution.
