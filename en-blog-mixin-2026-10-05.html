✦So What Is Feng Shui, Actually?

In one sentence: feng shui is the study of how mountains, water, roads, wind, and light shape the people inside a space.

Today's vocabulary calls it environmental psychology or evidence-based workplace design. The old vocabulary calls it feng shui. Same principles, different labels. An office entrance facing straight into elevator traffic leaves employees on edge and turnover high — the old texts call it an"open-mouth sha"; a modern consultant would talk about airflow and psychological pressure. Same phenomenon.

Real feng shui reads the landform — the actual terrain around a site. Which way a headquarters entrance should face cannot be decided by compass superstition alone; it must be determined by the full picture: hills, water, roads, trees, towers, neighboring buildings. The landform is the foundation. That is the hard skill passed down through my lineage, and it is what separates a practitioner from a fortune-teller.
