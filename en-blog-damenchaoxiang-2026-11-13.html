Wrong Facing on Your Company Door?
Three Years of Work, Down the Drain

The door is the company's mouth — face it wrong, and everything that comes in leaks out

A company I visited had good products, a good team, and a boss who arrived first and left last every day. Three years in, the bank account was still empty.

Clients walked in, looked around, and walked out. Deals died at the last step. Staff kept quitting. The boss asked me: is my fate just bad?

I said: don't blame fate yet. First, tell me — which way does your company door face?

🤔 The doubt: a door is just an entrance. South or north — how much difference can it make? And isn't the facing set by the boss's birth chart?

My answer is direct: the difference is four words — energy enters through the door. The door is the whole building's mouth. Whatever direction it faces, that is the energy the company takes in. Face it wrong and what comes in is not wealth but stale, hostile energy — and no matter how hard you work, you are pouring water into a bucket with no bottom. The Ba Zhai east-four / west-four lives rule — I don't use it. The door faces the whole building's energy, not one man's fate. If the boss changes, must the door turn too? Absurd.

✦How I Set the Facing on My Own House

My family's new house back home sits on Hai mountain, Si facing — a water formation. I compared the north–south facing against the Hai–Si facing myself, three ways: the hexagram, the landforms, and the energy principles. North–south lost. Hai–Si water formation won, all three methods agreeing, and only then did I settle it.

On the same plot, the house next door — Li Xiaoxia's house — faces Zi mountain, Wu direction. Every house faces different mountains, water, roads, and trees outside its door, so the facing has to be different. If even my own houses cannot share one facing, why should your company's door be settled by three words — "south is good"?

So what does decide a facing? A door's facing is never decided by compass luck alone or by a birth chart. It is decided by everything outside the door — mountains, water, roads, trees, towers, tall buildings. Whatever stands outside the door is what the door takes in.

✦The Three Most Common Mistakes

Mistake one: setting the facing by the boss's birth chart. East-four life faces east, west-four life faces west. The door faces the whole building's energy, not one person's fate.

Mistake two: chasing south and sunlight only. South is bright, yes — but if a main road rushes straight at the door from the south, or a power tower or chimney stands there, the light comes in and so does the killing energy. Look at what stands outside the door first, then decide the direction.

Mistake three: right facing, wrong interior. The door faces an auspicious direction, but inside there is a wall straight ahead, a long corridor running through, or a reception desk facing the elevator head-on. The good energy comes in and gets scattered.

✦Master Rain's Fix: Measure, Look, Decide, Adjust

Step 1: Measure

Take a compass on site and measure the sitting and facing. One degree off, and the fortune can flip — a facing is not approximate, it is exact to the degree.

Step 2: Look

Read the mountains, water, roads, trees, towers, and tall buildings outside the door. Where the harm sits, where the good sits — see it clearly before touching anything.

Step 3: Decide

Decide the facing, the door position, and the interior flow together. Facing, door, and flow are one energy path; decide them apart, and each one goes wrong.

Step 4: Adjust

If the sitting direction truly cannot change, adjust the door position, add a screen wall, change the flow — block the harm outside, draw the good energy in. Unable to turn the facing does not mean nothing can be done.

✦One Bottom Line: The Facing Must Be Measured On Site

Every building sits differently, and the environment outside every door is different. The facing has to be measured on site with a compass. For your own building, that can only be settled on site. I insist on on-site inspection and do not do feng shui by video: a photo cannot measure degrees, and it cannot show everything outside your door.

A door's facing cannot change your fate. But face it wrong, and the best fate is still water poured into a bottomless bucket. Three wasted years are not the tragedy. The tragedy is walking through the same door in year four.
