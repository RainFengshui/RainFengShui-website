Does One Feng Shui Fix Last Forever?
Feng Shui Can Expire

Heaven's timing moves, earth's conditions change, people change

"Master Rain, once the feng shui is done — is that it for life?" I get asked this constantly.

Others ask it more bluntly: "I had it done a few years ago. Why has it stopped working? Was I scammed?"

Here's the straight answer: you weren't scammed. You treated feng shui like a one-time product.

Feng shui can expire. Let me spell it out.

⏳ Reason 1: Heaven's Timing Moves — Luck Expires

Feng shui rests on three legs: heaven's timing, earth's conditions, and people. All three must hold.

Heaven's timing is the Three Cycles and Nine Periods: a 180-year grand cycle, with the dominant energy shifting every 20 years. Prosperity follows the period.

In 2024, we entered the Ninth Period of the Lower Cycle (the Li trigram reigns, 2024–2043). When the period turns, the directions and industries that thrived twenty years ago change completely. Add the annual flying stars — every year, fortune lands on different sectors.

The layout you set years ago was tuned to the timing of that year. The timing moved on; the layout didn't. Of course the effect fades. That's not the master's fault — that's the law of heaven's timing.

🏗️ Reason 2: Earth's Conditions Change — the Environment Expires

The house didn't change. Everything around it did.

A tower rises across the street — crushing sha. A straight road is built to your door — rushing sha. Construction starts next door — ground-breaking sha. The old tree at the compound gate gets cut down — the Azure Dragon is gone.

Cities reinvent themselves daily. The environment on the day of your adjustment and the environment three years later can be unrecognizable.

When earth's conditions change, the old layout is like expired medicine — same pill, different disease.

👨‍👩‍👧 Reason 3: People Change — Needs Expire

Human luck moves in ten-year chapters.

The year of your adjustment you were single; now you're married with kids. Then you worked for someone; now you run the company. Then your parents were hale; now their health sector needs support.

Same house — different people, different chapters, different needs.

Feng shui serves people. When people change, the feng shui must change with them.
