BUSINESS PAIN · TERRAIN IN FRONT OF THE DOOR

Your Company Door Faces a Downhill Slope?
Wealth Runs Downhill With It

Get the terrain wrong, and wealth walks in the door and straight back out

A company I visited sits at the top of a slope. Push the door open and the ground falls away in front of you, down to the street.

The boss told me: deals look good in the room and die on the way home. Staff leave after a few months. Money comes in and slips straight back out. Is my fate just bad?

I said: don't blame fate yet. Look at the ground outside your door first. Flat ground or a downhill slope — the difference is whether wealth can stay.

🤔 The doubt: what's wrong with a downhill road? Easy to drive, wide view — can a slope really affect money?

My answer is direct: energy follows the ground. Water runs downhill, and so does energy. A door facing straight down a slope is a door with a leak — energy walks in and slides straight back out. The view is good. The money does not stay.

✦Three Kinds of Slope

Gentle slope: the gradient is small and a flat patch in front of the door still catches the energy. It drains slowly.

Steep slope: the ground falls away the moment you step out. Energy rushes out in one go — clients walk downhill, money flows downhill. Nothing stays.

Long slope: not steep, but long and straight. Energy leaks the whole way down, like a pipe with a slow leak — the longer it runs, the more is lost.

How bad it is comes down to three numbers: gradient, slope length, and the width of the bright hall.

✦On Site, Three Things

First, the bright hall — the open ground in front of the door. It must be flat, wide, and able to gather energy. A sloping bright hall gathers nothing, like a tilted table.

Second, the water mouth. Energy behaves like water, and water runs downhill. A downhill door puts the water mouth at the low point, so wealth flows out with it. The old saying: energy rides the wind and scatters, and water stops it.

Third, the approach. Everyone climbs up from below, energy pushing uphill while people climb with effort. The moment they leave, it is downhill all the way — people leave fast, and money leaves fast with them.

✦Leave the Bright Hall Empty, So Energy Can Gather

A bright hall exists to gather energy. Leave the ground in front of the door open — greenery and open space, no heavy building, nothing packed down — so energy has somewhere to settle. Fill it up and pack it down, and energy has nowhere to land.

Water works the same way: it must embrace and rest. A pool gathers energy; a slope drains it. One holds, one lets go — exact opposites. In terrain, a small difference is a big difference, which is why I read the terrain first and everything else after.

✦The Fix: Block, Turn, Gather

Step 1: Block — Raise the Threshold, Build a Platform

Raise the threshold and lay a level platform in front of the door, so draining energy meets a barrier first. A threshold is not decoration — it is the first line keeping energy in.

Step 2: Turn — Set a Screen Wall, Bend the Energy Path

Put a screen wall just inside the door, so energy entering has to turn a corner, slow down, and stay. Energy that runs straight through never stays; energy that turns stops.

Step 3: Gather — Bring Water In to Hold the Energy

If the ground in front allows it, bring water in — a water feature — so the energy meets water and stops. Water holds energy; once energy stops, wealth stops with it. But where the water goes and how much depends on the water mouth and the terrain outside — water brought in wrong is worse than none.

✦One Bottom Line: Walk the Terrain

Gradient, slope length, bright-hall width, water-mouth position — no photo can show them, no video can measure them. I insist on on-site inspection and do not do feng shui by video.

And one plain truth: avoiding a downhill door when choosing costs far less than fixing one after moving in. Adjusting is a remedy; choosing well is the foundation.

A downhill slope in front of the door is not a death sentence — but it is not a small thing either. Only when energy can stay can wealth stay.
