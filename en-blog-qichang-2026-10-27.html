The Office Feels Dead,
Nobody Has Any Drive?

Do not blame people first. Look at the field — revive the field, and people revive

Walk in at nine and it feels like a room nobody opened a window in: lights on, people flat. Clock in, get water, sit down, drift. Work runs on willpower alone.

The boss blames lazy staff, blames young people with no drive. But the same people, in a different office, at a different desk, get their drive back. The people did not change — the field did.

Energy is alive. Live energy, live people; dead energy, flat people. When the office's energy dies, raises do not bring drive back.

Do not blame people first. Look at the field. Revive the field, and people revive.

🥀 Three Things That Kill an Office's Energy

First: dim light, stale air — no sunlight all day, air that never moves; energy stagnates in the room. People in stagnant energy feel foggy, drowsier by the hour; by afternoon the whole office is yawning.

Second: desks backed to doors, facing walls — back to a corridor, to a door, never settled; face a corner, a pillar, pressure on the chest. One day in that seat costs three days of tiredness.

Third: clutter, dead plants, dusty corners — a warehouse-style office, dead plants, corners never cleaned. Enough dead things and the energy dies; dead energy, flat people.

📉 Real Case: A Round of Raises, People Still Left

A tech company with turnover through the roof. The owner assumed pay was the problem, raised salaries a round, and people still left.

On site: a north-facing office that never saw sun, desks backed to the entrance, corners piled with old equipment and dead plants. People sat all day in stagnant, pressured, dead energy — no salary makes up for that.

The fixes: brighter warm lighting, desks turned to solid walls, dead corners cleared, live plants in. Two months later the owner said: the office feels like a different place, nobody dozes after lunch. The pay did not suddenly become enough — the energy came alive.

People will not stay? Before raising pay again, check whether the office's energy is still alive.

✦Master Rain's Fixes

First: bring in light and air — sunlight where possible, warm light where not

Air must move. Stagnant energy is the root of flatness; cut the root and people wake up.

Second: desks need backing — solid wall behind, open space in front

Backed by a solid wall, supported at the side, sitting steady settles the mind; open in front, no corners, no pillars. A settled mind brings drive back.

Third: clear and revive — clear dead corners, replace dead plants, add live ones

Clear dead corners on a schedule, replace dead plants, add live ones. A clean field lets energy move; moving energy gives people drive.

✦Do the Math

What does one resignation cost — hiring, training, handover? What does a whole office running at half speed every afternoon cost over a year?

Raises to keep people are money spent every year; adjusting the field is money spent once. Morale's bill runs bigger than the payroll — payroll you can see; the daily cost of low output and resignations runs every day, it just never gets added up as one bill.

A living field keeps people; kept people earn the money back. Anyone can do this math.
