Is It Broken?

Feng shui is not a painkiller. Good energy, like bad energy, needs time to work.

"Master, how soon will it work? Will my luck turn this month?"

Almost every client asks. My answer is blunt: if you treat feng shui like magic — or like a painkiller — you will be disappointed.

Feng shui adjusts your long-term energy field and trajectory. It is not an adrenaline shot. Adjusted today, jackpot tomorrow — that is not feng shui. That is a daydream.

✦First, Consider the Reverse: How Long Does Bad Feng Shui Take to Hurt You?

My own lesson, in eight Chinese characters: feng shui made me — and feng shui broke me.

When I moved into a mansion with terrible feng shui, I did not go broke overnight. It took two years — a slow bleed that cost me millions of dollars.

Bad feng shui does not bankrupt you the next morning. It works like a chronic illness, draining your wealth, your health, and your judgment day by day, until one morning you realize the damage is done.

Good feng shui works the same way in reverse. It pulls you off the downhill road and onto an uphill one — and that takes time.

To be fair, some sensitive people feel a shift within a week — but that is the exception, not the standard. Even medicine requires a full course of treatment. If you won't grant feng shui a single course, not even a deity could help you.
